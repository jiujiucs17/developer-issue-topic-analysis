For torch.nn.CrossEntropyLoss, if weight argument is specified, the loss is computed as:
\text{loss} = \frac{\sum^{N}{i=1} loss(i, class[i])}{\sum^{N}{i=1} weight[class[i]]}
The normalization term in the denominator is computed given the batch of data.
This will make the results of reduction=mean and the results of reduction=sum/reduction=none  inconsistent.
